Account deletion

Delete my Nobel Export Company account

Nobel Export Company accounts belong to Nobel Export Company's staff and invited clients.

How to ask

  1. Tell the factory office (the General Manager), or
  2. Email apps@724.uk.com with the subject “Delete my Nobel Export Company account”, giving your name and the phone number on your account.

We confirm who you are before deleting anything, and we reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login and PIN or password
  • Your face template, voice sample and photos
  • Your phone number and WhatsApp messages

What is kept, and for how long

Attendance, salary, advance and payslip records are kept by Nobel Export Company for as long as Indian wage, tax and employment law requires, and are then deleted.
