Privacy policy

Nobel Export Company

Last updated 18 November 2026

Nobel Export Company is a private workplace app. Accounts are created only by Nobel Export Company for its own staff, managers and invited business clients.

Who is responsible

Data fiduciary (controller)Nobel Export Company, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India — the employer that decides what the app is used for. Contact: nobel151013@gmail.com
App publisher and service providerBVBAGRI FOODS CORPORATION LIMITED, United Kingdom (company number 16666256). We build and run the app for Nobel Export Company and use the data only on its instructions. Contact: apps@724.uk.com

What the app collects, and why

Staff recordName (in English and Tamil), photo, phone number, department, shift, joining date, pay details and bank details.To employ you, plan work and pay you.
Face and voice checkA face template (a set of numbers made from photos of your face), a short voice sample, and photos taken at the factory gate tablet or gate cameras.To record attendance — who came in and went out, and when — and to stop anyone marking attendance for someone else.
LocationYour device's location at the moment you check in or out.To confirm you were at the factory. It is not tracked at other times.
Work recordsAttendance times, time spent out of the factory, extra work, leave, production entries, salary advances (with your signature) and payslips.To run the factory and to work out pay correctly.
MessagesWhatsApp messages and voice notes exchanged with the factory's WhatsApp number.To ask about and record reasons (for example, time out of the factory) and to send reminders.
VisitorsPhotos of people at the factory gate who are not staff, with the time.Security of the factory and its staff.
Business clientsContact details, orders and messages.To take and deliver orders.

The app does not show advertising and does not sell or share personal data for marketing.

Who else handles the data

Only service providers that the app needs in order to work, each under contract:

  • Render — hosts the app and its database.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) through Kapso — sends and receives WhatsApp messages.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — reads documents and photos that managers upload (for example a staff sheet) and writes Tamil versions of names.

These providers may process data outside India, including in the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United States.

How long it is kept

Personal data is kept while you work with Nobel Export Company and afterwards only for as long as it is needed for pay, tax and employment records under Indian law. Gate photos of visitors are kept only as long as they are needed for security.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask for a summary of your data, ask for it to be corrected or erased, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Ask the factory office, or email nobel151013@gmail.com or apps@724.uk.com. To delete your account, see delete my account.

Children

The app is for adults only. It is not used by anyone under 18.

Security

Each person sees only what their role allows. Passwords and PINs are stored scrambled (hashed), all connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and the factory owner approves important changes.
